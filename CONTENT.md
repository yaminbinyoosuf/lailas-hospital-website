Our Heritage in Chemmad Since 1994
##
Three Decades of “Humane Care; Divine Cure” in Malappuram
Founded in 1994 in Chemmad when specialized maternal and emergency medical facilities were scarce in the Tirurangadi region, Lailas Hospital and IVF Centre has grown into one of Malappuram’s most trusted family healthcare institutions.
In 1996 , we pioneered the locality’s first diagnostic Ultrasound Scanning facility. Today, having welcomed over 70,000+ babies with among the lowest caesarean and neonatal mortality rates in the region, we continue to serve generations of families with affordable, ethical, and compassionate clinical excellence.

## Frequently Asked Questions
Quick answers for patients visiting Lailas Hospital, Chemmad
How can I book an OPD token or IVF consultation?
You can book directly using the Book Consultation button on this website, message our WhatsApp desk at +91 8606 800 999 , or call our reception at 0494 2463377 / 2464193 .
Are Emergency, Pharmacy, and Lab services open on Sundays?
Yes. Our Emergency & Casualty department, Labour Room, Clinical Laboratory, and In-House Pharmacy operate 24 hours a day, 7 days a week , including Sundays and holidays.
When should we visit the IVF & Fertility Centre?
Couples trying to conceive for over a year (or 6 months if the female partner is over 35), as well as individuals with irregular cycles, PCOS, or prior pregnancy losses, can schedule a confidential evaluation any day from Monday to Saturday.
Is parking available at the hospital in Chemmad town?
Yes, Lailas Hospital has a dedicated, wheelchair-accessible parking facility along Chemmad–Thalappara / Kozhikode Road with easy access to the emergency bay and main OPD block.
